About Us

A Mother-Daughter Dream

Welcome to Ujjwala & Vaishnavi's Home Bakery! We're a passionate mother-daughter duo who turned our love for baking into a sweet journey of creating magical moments for your special occasions.

Every cake that comes out of our kitchen is made with fresh, premium ingredients and a whole lot of love. We believe in the power of homemade goodness and personalized service that makes each creation uniquely yours.

From whimsical butterfly cakes to adventurous airplane designs, we specialize in bringing your themed cake dreams to life. And the best part? We deliver fresh, beautiful cakes right to your doorstep!

Frequently Asked Questions

🎂 How far in advance should I order?
We recommend ordering at least 3-5 days in advance for custom themed cakes to ensure we have enough time to create your perfect design. Rush orders may be accommodated based on availability.
🚚 What areas do you deliver to?
We offer free home delivery within a 10km radius. For areas beyond that, a small delivery charge may apply. Contact us to check if we deliver to your location!
🎨 Can I customize the design and flavors?
Absolutely! We love bringing your unique visions to life. From the design and colors to flavors and dietary requirements, we work with you to create exactly what you want.
💰 What are your prices?
Prices vary based on size, complexity, and customization. Contact us with your requirements and we'll provide you with a detailed quote. We offer great value for premium quality!
